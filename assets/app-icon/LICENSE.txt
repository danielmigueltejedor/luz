Luz app icon (lightcraft.svg, lightcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of Louis Daguerre cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Louis Daguerre 2.jpg", from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Louis_Daguerre_2.jpg).
Status of the source: public domain.
